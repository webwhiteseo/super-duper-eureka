ORE FACTORY - 20 DROPPER MINES
==============================
Mines 1-5 (folder mine/):  Frost Wyrm, Emerald Wyvern, Crimson Drake, Astral Orrery, Cosmic Drake (fusion)
                           -> colour them with mine/MineLighting.lua
Mines 6-20 (folder mines20/): each folder has its own <Name>_RobloxSetup.lua

  6 Magma Golem Forge      11 Void Rift              16 Sandstinger (scorpion)
  7 Kraken Depths          12 Phoenix Nest           17 Corsair Cannon (pirate ship)
  8 Tempest Coil           13 Sakura Shrine          18 Fire & Ice Serpents (Frost Wyrm + Crimson Drake fusion)
  9 Sun Pyramid            14 Ore Express (train)    19 Titan Drill
 10 Glowshroom Grove       15 Clockwork Owl          20 Astral Owl (Clockwork Owl + Astral Orrery fusion)

NO BASE: mines 6-20 have no display plinth - they sit straight on the ground, so drop them onto your map.
(Corsair Cannon keeps a thin "SeaWater" pool so the ship floats - delete that part if you have your own water.)
Want the plinth back? Re-run the script with --base.

PUT ONE IN ROBLOX STUDIO (mines 6-20)
1. Home > Import 3D > pick <Name>_Roblox.fbx
   - Scale Unit = Stud, UNTICK "Import as single mesh", tick Anchored. Click Import.
2. Click the imported model in the Explorer so it is selected.
3. View > Command Bar. Open <Name>_RobloxSetup.lua, copy ALL of it, paste into the Command Bar, press Enter.
4. View > Output shows what it did. The setup:
   - colours every part + sets Roblox materials (Neon glows, Glass, Metal, etc.)
   - adds PointLights to glowing parts
   - turns the ore cube into an invisible "Drop" part and adds a working "Dropper" script
5. Press Play: ore drops from the drop point every 2 seconds.
   Change speed/value: select the model > Properties > Attributes (DropInterval, OreValue, OreSize).

EDIT IN BLENDER
- Open <Name>.blend, or re-run the script:  python3 <script>.py -- --out . --render
- Every script needs mine_kit.py (in mines20/, and inside each zip) next to it or one folder up.
